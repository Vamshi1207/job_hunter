# LinkedIn cold DM template

## Hard rules
- ≤ 60 words
- Opens with one specific, verifiable thing about THE PERSON or company (not "I love your mission")
- One sentence on user's strongest signal (anchor project + verified metric)
- One sentence soft ask: "Would you have 15 min for a chat?" or "Could I send you my CV?"
- No emojis
- No "Hope this finds you well"
- No "I came across your profile"
- Sign-off per config.yaml outreach.signoff

## Default skeleton

> Hi {{FIRST_NAME}},
>
> {{SPECIFIC_HOOK}} — {{ONE_LINE_TIE_TO_THEIR_WORK}}.
>
> {{ONE_LINE_USER_SIGNAL}}. {{ONE_LINE_WHY_FIT_THIS_COMPANY}}.
>
> Would you have 15 min for a quick chat about {{ROLE_OR_TEAM}}?
>
> {{SIGNOFF}}

## Hook examples (replace `{{SPECIFIC_HOOK}}`)

✅ Good (specific, verifiable):
- "Saw your interview on [Sifted / podcast] about <company>'s APAC push"
- "Read your Series C announcement last month"
- "Watched your demo at <event> on <feature>"
- "Saw your blog post on <specific-engineering-decision>"

❌ Bad (generic, ignored by recipients):
- "Saw your impressive profile"
- "I'm a big fan of your work"
- "Your company is doing amazing things"
- "Hope this message finds you well"

## When to send vs not

- **Send when:** You can find a specific recent thing about the person (talk, post, blog) that genuinely interested you, AND they're at a company on your high-priority target list.
- **Don't send when:** You can't think of anything specific to open with — the cold DM will read as spam. Better to apply through careers page than to send a generic DM.
