# "Why I fit" — 3-bullet template

For pasting into application form free-text fields ("Why are you interested in this role?", "What makes you a good fit?").

## Hard rules

- 3 bullets
- Each ≤ 25 words
- Each starts with a verb
- Each ties to ONE specific JD requirement
- No "I'm passionate about", "I thrive in"
- Use verified accomplishments only — no invented metrics

## Skeleton

- {{VERB}} {{VERIFIED_EVIDENCE}} → {{TIE_TO_JD_REQ_1}}.
- {{VERB}} {{VERIFIED_EVIDENCE}} → {{TIE_TO_JD_REQ_2}}.
- {{VERB}} {{VERIFIED_EVIDENCE}} → {{TIE_TO_JD_REQ_3}}.

## Example (for a Growth Generalist role at a B2B AI startup)

- Took an iOS app from zero to 800+ paying subscribers entirely through a self-built 8K-subscriber newsletter — owned hook, distribution, conversion end to end.
- Iterated subscription pricing and product positioning weekly against real subscriber feedback on the UK App Store.
- Built distribution in a non-English-native context — directly relevant to <company>'s multilingual user base.
